The Course

UX & Product Design, taught the way studios actually work.

Most courses teach tools. This one teaches judgment — how to research a real problem, structure an interface around it, and defend your decisions in a portfolio review. Every weekend ends with something shipped, not just slides.

Weekends 1–2

UX Foundations & Research

User research methods, personas, journey mapping and problem framing — grounded in a live brief, not a textbook example.

Weekends 3–5

Wireframing & Prototyping in Figma

Information architecture, low-fi to high-fi wireframes, interactive prototypes and a design system built from scratch.

Weekend 6

Visual & Interaction Design

Typography, layout systems, motion and micro-interactions — the craft details that separate usable from memorable.

Weekends 7–8

Capstone & Portfolio

Ship one end-to-end case study, then package it — and yourself — into a portfolio and story ready for interviews or clients.
